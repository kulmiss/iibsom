# Jiji study: ideas for iibsom (2026-11-17)

**How this was made.** jiji.com.et answered every visit from our cloud browser with a Cloudflare "are you a bot?" page. We do not get around such checks, so we could not click through jiji.com.et itself. The list below comes from Jiji's own public pages on its other country sites (About / How to sell, Safety tips, FAQ), news articles about Jiji, and well-known parts of Jiji's site. **We take ideas only.** iibsom keeps its own look, colours, logo, icons, words and layout.

Value = how much it helps people in Somalia (1–10). Effort: S = hours, M = a day, L = several days.
