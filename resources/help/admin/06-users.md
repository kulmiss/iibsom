# Users

Customers who signed in with Google or with their email and password.

- Tap a user to see their ads, when they joined and how many reports they made.
- **Ban:** write a reason. They are signed out and cannot sign in again. You can also take down their live and waiting ads, and block their phone number, in the same step.
- **Unban:** they can sign in again. Their old ads stay rejected.
- **Block a phone number:** nobody can use that number in a profile. Use it for known scammers.
- **Phone confirmed** (no SMS): call or WhatsApp the seller's number. When the person answers from that number, open the user and tap **Mark phone confirmed**. Sellers can also tap **Confirm my phone number** on their account page: they get a code like `IIB-7KQ3M` and send it to the iibsom WhatsApp number (Settings → Site rules → Emails). Type the code in the Users search (or the top Search) to find them, check the message came from the number on the account, then tick it. Every tick and removal is in the Activity log. The tick goes away by itself if the seller changes the number.
