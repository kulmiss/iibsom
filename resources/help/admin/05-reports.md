# Reports

Ads that people reported (scam, wrong category, already sold, not allowed). Waiting reports come first, and among them **the ads with the most reports come to the top** (a red "N reports on this ad" label).

- **Reported by:** a customer's name, or **Guest** (someone who was not signed in, when the switch "Report an ad without signing in" is ON). "sent 3×" means the same guest report came 3 times; it is counted once in the list.
- **Contact:** a phone or email the guest left so you can answer. It is removed 30 days after the report is handled.
- **Take ad down:** the report was right. The ad leaves the site and all waiting reports on it close.
- **Dismiss:** the report was wrong. The ad stays.

Many reports against one seller? Open **Users** and look at their other ads.
