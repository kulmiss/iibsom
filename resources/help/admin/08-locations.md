# Cities and districts

Only cities that are ON can be chosen when posting and appear on the site.

- A new city starts OFF. Switch it ON when you are ready. Hargeisa, Burao, Berbera and Borama were added OFF (PR 111).
- **Districts** (for example Hodan, Wadajir or KM4): sellers can pick one (optional). Buyers filter by district on **Property** and **Vehicles** lists once they choose a city (switch "District filter on every list" shows it everywhere).
- Tap **N districts** next to a city to manage them: **add** (English + Somali name), **edit** (tap the name), move **up/down** to change the order, switch **off** the ones you don't need, **delete** one that no ad uses yet.
- A yellow **check names** mark = a name from our starting list that a native speaker has not checked yet. Saving the names (or **Names are right**) removes the mark. The same names are in the Somali review sheet.
