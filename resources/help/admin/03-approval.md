# Waiting for approval

Every new or changed ad waits here before buyers can see it (switch: **Admin approval**).

- Tap an ad to see all photos and details.
- **Approve:** the ad goes live at once.
- **Reject:** write a short reason. The seller sees it in **My ads** and can fix the ad.
- Tick many ads to approve or reject them at once.
- **Edited** means the seller changed an ad that was already approved. You see the old and new text side by side, so you only check what changed.

Reject when: wrong category, fake or stolen photos, no real price, phone numbers or links in the text, things that are not allowed.
