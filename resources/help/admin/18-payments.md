# Payments (paid boosts)

Phase 2: only shown when the **Paid boosts** switch is ON. Only the Super Admin can check payments.

Sellers boost a live ad from **My ads → Boost**: **Top** (7 days), **VIP** (14 days) or **Diamond** (30 days). They pay with EVC Plus, Zaad, Sahal or eDahab to your merchant number, then send the transaction ID and a screenshot of the payment SMS. Boosted ads show first in lists with a label.

1. Open your mobile-money account and check that the money **really arrived**: same amount, same transaction ID.
2. **Approve:** the boost starts at once (or adds days if the ad is already boosted). The money is written in the books.
3. **Reject** with a reason when the money did not arrive or does not match. No boost starts.

One transaction ID can only be used once, and a payment can only be decided once.

## Test mode

Settings → General → **Payments for boosts**. While **Test mode** is ON, sellers see "Do not send real money", and payments are marked **Test** and kept apart from the real books. Fill in your real merchant numbers first, then turn test mode OFF. An empty number means that way to pay is not offered.

## The books

The top of the page shows the money per account (real and test apart): `cash:evc_plus` is money received by EVC Plus, `revenue:boosts` is what boosts earned. The two always match. If the page says the books do not balance, contact your developer.
