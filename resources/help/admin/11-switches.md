# Feature switches

One switch for every feature. OFF means hidden everywhere, as if it never existed. Data is kept, so you can switch it ON again later.

- Switches are in **sections** by what they change (Home page, Ads and ad pages, Posting an ad, …). Press a section's title to open or close it; **Open all** / **Close all** at the top. This browser remembers which sections you closed.
- At the top: **Find a switch** (words from the name or the description), **Show** (all, only ON, only OFF, changed by you) and **Sort** (usual order, A–Z, ON first, OFF first). While you search or filter, every section with a match is open.
- The **Phase 2** and **Phase 3** sections are at the end. Most of them stay OFF until you decide; Saved ads, Saved searches, Ad statistics, Verified badge, Auto-post and Help for buyers abroad are ON for launch.
- **Launch setup** (button at the top) sets the recommended ON/OFF for the first days of the live site in one click. It first shows the list of what would change and why; only those switches change, and it is saved in the Activity log. It also turns **demo data OFF**, so use it on the live site.
- A yellow **Needs Chat (OFF)** (or another name) means: this switch is ON, but it shows nothing until that other switch is ON.
- Each switch has an easy description. Read it before you change it.
- Every change is saved in the **Activity log**.
