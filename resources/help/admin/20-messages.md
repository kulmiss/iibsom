# Messages (contact form)

Only shown when the **Contact form** switch is ON. Super Admin and Support can use it.

Customers write to you from **Contact us** (footer, or `/contact`): their name, email, what it is about and the message. No IP address or cookie is kept.

- **To answer:** new messages, newest first. The number in the tab is how many are waiting.
- **Answer by email:** opens your email program with the customer's address and the topic.
- **Done:** after you answered. **Not done** moves it back.

Robots and floods are stopped automatically (hidden trap field, time check, limits). Messages are deleted after 180 days.
