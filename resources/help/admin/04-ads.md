# Ads

Every ad on the site: live, waiting, rejected, sold and ended.

- Search by title, or filter by status and category.
- Tap an ad to see it all and its **History** (who approved, rejected or edited it).
- **Edit:** fix a small mistake yourself in the title, description or price. Your change is saved as approved.
- **Delete:** removes the ad from the site. Use **Reject** instead when the seller should fix it.

## Drugs

Drugs (khat, cannabis, narcotics, prescription medicines, drug tools) are not allowed. Filter **Drugs** shows the ads sent back for checking by the drug check (red **Drugs** badge; the panel names the word) and ads reported as drugs. If it is a false alarm (a normal word), **Approve** it: the mark goes away. Otherwise **Reject** with "Drugs are not allowed".
