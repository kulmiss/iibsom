# Ratings (seller ratings)

Phase 3: only shown when the **Seller ratings** switch is ON (it needs **In-website chat** ON too). Super Admin and Moderators can use it.

Buyers give a seller 1 to 5 stars and an optional short comment from the chat. They can only do it **after the seller has answered them**, so every rating comes from a real conversation. One rating per chat; the buyer can change it.

The seller's average and number of ratings show next to their name on ad pages and shop pages, with a link to all their ratings.

- **Shown / Hidden:** ratings on the website, and ones you hid.
- **Stars filter:** look at 1-star ratings first: most problems are there.
- **Hide:** pick or type a reason. The rating disappears and the seller's stars are counted again at once. The buyer sees that it was hidden.
- **Show again:** undo a hide.

Hide ratings with rude words, personal details (phone numbers), fake ratings from friends, or ones about something else. Don't hide a fair bad rating just because the seller asks.
