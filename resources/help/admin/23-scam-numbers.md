# Scam numbers

A list of phone numbers your team checked and marked **"Reported for scams"**. Only staff can add a number (Super Admin and Moderator); nothing is ever added by itself.

- **Mark a number:** tap **Mark a number**, type the number, a short **reason** and the **proof** (report numbers, what buyers sent, payment numbers). Mark a number only after you checked the reports.
- What happens then:
  - that number cannot post or renew ads (also when it is written inside the ad text);
  - its live ads go back to **Waiting for approval** and cannot be approved while the number is on the list;
  - buyers who **search** for the number, or check it on the **Check a phone number** page, see "Reported for scams". They **never** see a name, the proof, or who marked it;
  - the **Watchdog** sends an alert when a listed number tries to post.
- **Review requests:** the owner of a number can ask for a review on the Check a phone number page. Read the message, then **Accept** (the number comes off the list) or **Refuse** (it stays). Write a short note: it is saved in the Activity log. Their contact is deleted 30 days after you decide.
- **Take off the list:** if it was a mistake, tap **Take off the list** and write why. Ads that wait stay waiting until you approve them.

Every mark, removal and decision is in the **Activity log**. The switch is **Scam number warning list** (Feature switches → Safety and reports).
