# Staff

People who can use this admin.

- **Add staff:** type their email and pick a role. You get a first password: give it to them privately (not by email). You will not see it again.
- At their first sign-in they set up 2FA with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator…).
- **Reset 2FA:** for someone who lost their phone. They set it up again at next sign-in.
- **Reset password:** gives a new one-time password for someone who forgot theirs.
- **Change role** or **Remove from staff** at any time.

Give each person the smallest role that lets them do their job.
