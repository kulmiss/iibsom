# Start here

This guide explains every page of the admin in easy words. Tap **?** next to a page title to open the part about that page.

## First sign-in

A 5-step setup asks the main questions: languages, ad rules, cities, emails and demo data. Press **Later** to do it another time. You can run it again from Settings → General.

## Every day (10 minutes)

1. **Waiting for approval:** approve good ads, reject bad ones with a reason.
2. **Reports:** look at ads people reported.
3. **Dashboard:** check that new ads and users keep coming.

## Before the site goes live

- Turn **Show demo data** OFF and delete the demo data (Settings → Demo data).
- Check the About, Terms and Privacy pages (Pages).
- Set the **Help email for customers** and the **Weekly summary email** (Settings → General).
- Check **System health**: nothing should say MISSING.

## Who can do what

- **Super Admin:** everything.
- **Moderator:** ads, reports, users and the activity log.
- **Support:** can look at ads, reports and users, but not change them.

Everyone signs in with a password **and** a 6-digit code from an authenticator app (2FA). Staff are signed out after 60 minutes without activity.
