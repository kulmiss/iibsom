# How we keep you safe

We want every buyer and seller to feel safe on iibsom. This is what we do, and what you can do.

## What iibsom does

- **:checked_line
- **Your number is not in the page.** It shows only after a buyer taps **Show number**, so robots cannot collect it.
- **Bad ads are stopped.** Ads with rude words, web links or drugs (khat, cannabis, pills and similar) are refused when they are posted or changed. Chat messages about drugs are not sent.
- **Anyone can report.** Every ad has a **Report this ad** button, also for people who are not signed in. We check every report.
- **Cheats are removed.** People who break the rules are banned, their ads are taken down and their phone number is blocked.
- **We keep little about you.** We do not keep the internet address (IP) of visitors who are not signed in. Saved history like "Recently viewed" stays on your own phone.
- **Our staff sign in with two steps** (a password and a code from their phone).

## What you can do

1. Meet in a busy, public place in daylight. Bring a friend for big buys.
2. See and check the item before you pay. **Never send money first** (EVC Plus, Zaad, Sahal, eDahab).
3. iibsom will **never** call or message you to ask for a code, a password or money.
4. If something feels wrong, stop and tap **Report this ad**.
