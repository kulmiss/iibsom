# How to sell fast on iibsom

Selling on iibsom is free. These 6 steps help buyers find your ad and trust you.

1. **Post your ad.** Tap **Sell**, choose the category and fill in the details. Our team checks every ad before it goes live. [How to post an ad](:help_url/post-an-ad)
2. **Take good photos.** Daylight, a clean background, the whole item and close-ups. Up to :max_photos photos; the first one is the main photo. [Good photos](:help_url/good-photos)
3. **Give a fair price.** Look at similar ads first. Ads with a price get more calls than "Negotiable".
4. **Write the important details.** Year, size, condition, what is included. Buyers skip ads that leave them guessing.
5. **Answer quickly.** Keep your phone on and reply to calls and messages. Sellers who answer fast sell faster.
6. **Meet safely.** Meet in a busy public place, and never send money first. [Safety tips](:safety_url)

When it is sold, press **Mark as sold** in **My ads**, so buyers stop calling. Ads end after :expiry_days days; you can renew them in **My ads**.
