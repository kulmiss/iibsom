# Posting rules

We check every ad before it goes live. Follow these rules and your ad goes live faster.

## Every ad must

- Be a **real item or service** that you have now, in Somalia.
- Have **your own photos** of the item. No photos from the internet.
- Be in the **right category** and the right city.
- Have a **real price** in US dollars (or tick "Negotiable").
- Be **one ad for one item**. Do not post the same thing again: renew or move the old ad up instead.

## Not allowed

- Weapons, bullets and anything made to hurt people.
- Drugs, alcohol, medicines that need a doctor's paper.
- Stolen things, fake or copied brands, fake documents, ID cards or passports.
- Animals that are protected by law, and wild animals.
- Adult content, or anything that insults a person, clan or religion.
- Jobs or "business offers" that ask you to pay first.
- Links to other websites, and asking buyers to pay before they see the item.

## Why an ad is not approved

- **Photos are not clear or not real:** add your own clear photos in daylight.
- **Wrong category:** choose the category that fits best.
- **Price is missing or not real:** write the real price.
- **Duplicate ad:** you already have the same ad. Use that one.

Open **My ads** to see the reason, fix the ad and save it. We check it again. Breaking the rules many times can block your account.
