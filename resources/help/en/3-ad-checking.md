# Why is my ad waiting?

Every new ad is checked by our team, so buyers see real ads.

- **Waiting for approval:** we have not checked it yet. You do not need to do anything.
- **Live:** everyone can see your ad.
- **Rejected:** open **My ads** to see why. Fix the ad and save it. We will check it again.

When you change a live ad, it is checked again before the new version shows.
