# How to buy on iibsom

1. **Find it.** Search, or open a category and use the filters (price, city, condition). Save a search to hear about new ads.
2. **Look closely.** Read the whole ad and look at every photo. Check the seller: how long they are on iibsom and their stars.
3. **Ask.** Tap **Show number** to call or WhatsApp, or use the chat if it is on. Ask about anything that is not clear. You can also make an offer.
4. **Meet and check.** Meet in a busy, public place in daylight. See the item and check that it works before you pay.
5. **Pay safely.** Pay only when you have the item. Never send money first (EVC Plus, Zaad, Sahal, eDahab) for something you have not seen.
6. **Something wrong?** Tap **Report this ad**. We check every report.
