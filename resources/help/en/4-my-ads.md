# Edit, sold, renew or delete

Open **Account** → **My ads**.

- **Edit:** change the photos, text or price.
- **Mark as sold:** the ad leaves the site and buyers stop calling you.
- **Renew:** ads end after :expiry_days days. **Renew** shows :renew_days days before the end and gives your ad :expiry_days more days.
- **Delete:** the ad is removed for good.
