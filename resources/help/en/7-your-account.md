# Your account

- **Sign in:** tap **Sign in**, then choose your Google account (we never see your Google password) or use your email and password.
- **New with email:** tap **Create an account**, then open the link we email you before your first sign-in. No email? Look in spam.
- **Forgot your password:** on the sign-in page tap **Forgot password?** and we email you a link (it works for 60 minutes).
- **Change your name, city or phone:** **Account** → **Edit profile**.
- **Your phone number:** buyers see it on your ads after they tap **Show phone number**.
- **Language:** tap **English** or **Soomaali** at the top of any page.
- **Delete your account:** write to us and we will remove your account and your ads. Our email is at the bottom of the [Help page](:help_url).
