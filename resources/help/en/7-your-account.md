# Your account

- **Sign in:** tap **Sign in** and choose your Google account. We never see your Google password.
- **Change your name, city or phone:** **Account** → **Edit profile**.
- **Your phone number:** buyers see it on your ads after they tap **Show phone number**.
- **Language:** tap **English** or **Soomaali** at the top of any page.
- **Delete your account:** write to us and we will remove your account and your ads. Our email is at the bottom of the [Help page](:help_url).
