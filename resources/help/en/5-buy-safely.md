# Buy safely

- Meet in a busy, public place in daylight.
- See the item before you pay. Check that it works.
- Never send money first (EVC Plus, Zaad, Sahal, eDahab) for something you have not seen.
- For cars, houses and land, check the papers and who really owns it.
- A price that is much lower than normal is a warning sign.
