# Good photos sell faster

- Take photos in daylight, near a window or outside.
- Show the whole item first, then the details and any damage.
- Use your own photos. Photos copied from the internet may be refused.
- No phone numbers or writing on the photos. Buyers see your number on the ad page.
- Drag a photo to change the order. The first photo shows in the lists.

We remove hidden data (like the place where the photo was taken) from every photo.
