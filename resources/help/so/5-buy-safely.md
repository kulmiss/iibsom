# Si ammaan ah wax u iibso

- Ku kulma meel dadweyne oo mashquul ah, maalinnimo.
- Arag alaabta ka hor intaadan lacag bixin. Hubi inay shaqeyso.
- Weligaa lacag hore ha dirin (EVC Plus, Zaad, Sahal, eDahab) wax aadan arag.
- Baabuurta, guryaha iyo dhulka, hubi waraaqaha iyo cidda dhab ahaan leh.
- Qiime aad uga hooseeya sida caadiga ah waa calaamad digniin ah.
